The project, 2026

How Acurion came together.

From a compliance workspace to live detection, one release at a time.

  1. 13 Oct
    The workspace

    Customers and KYC, screening, rules and simulation, and cases in one place.

  2. 15 Oct
    Getting around

    A floating dock, a workspace map and the transaction activity calendar.

  3. 13 Nov
    Explained and protected

    Model alerts with their reasons; an encrypted workspace, a verifiable audit trail and signed reports.

  4. 15 Nov
    Acurion K7 goes live

    Real-time detection from the host computer, and dataset uploads scored by the core model.

  5. 16 Nov
    Asking, not guessing

    Every upload gets a column check before scoring, and pop-ups show what is happening.

  6. 24 Nov
    A new identity

    The Acurion logo and the calm navy and teal look.

  7. 4 Dec
    Innovation Fair

    Acurion shown at Fairmont The Palm, Dubai.
