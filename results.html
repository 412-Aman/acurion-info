01 Reading the numbers

A focused queue, not a complete one.

High precision keeps an analyst’s time on alerts that matter. A recall of 0.40 means K7 on its own misses many laundering transfers, which is one reason uploaded datasets and written rules sit beside it.

  • Scores are rankings

    A score from 0 to 1 orders activity for review. It is not the probability that laundering happened.

  • Simulated data

    The figures come from IBM’s simulated payments. They do not promise the same results on a real bank’s data.

  • A recorded stream

    K7 replays a recorded stream of payments for demonstration. It is not connected to a live bank.

03 Responsible use

Technology supports decisions. People remain accountable.

Acurion is an investigation-support tool. Every alert it raises waits for a person.

Human review

Evidence-led reporting
