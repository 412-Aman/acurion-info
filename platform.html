01 Acurion K7

Real-time monitoring that says why.

K7 checks each payment as it arrives, against the previous 14 days of that account’s activity. Three trained models score it together, and every alert carries its reasons in plain language.

  • Start and pause from any browser, for reviewers and admins
  • Progress and the newest alerts live in the header pill
  • On validation data, 9 in 10 of its alerts were laundering
How K7 was measured
02 Dataset analysis

Bring your own bank file.

Upload a CSV in whatever layout your bank exports. Acurion matches the columns to what it needs and asks you to confirm them before anything is scored.

  • Best guesses are marked, and nothing is scored until you confirm
  • Ambiguous dates like 03/09 are asked about, never guessed
  • Alerts and laundering networks are shared with the whole team
03 Rules engine

Written checks anyone can read.

Alongside the models, a rules engine checks the case workspace’s customers and payments. Every result is Pass, Flag or Block, with the rule that fired.

  • Thresholds, income multiples, velocity and structuring
  • Incomplete KYC, PEP and confirmed sanctions matches
  • Results are recommendations; a person decides

04 Explainable alerts

Every alert shows its working.

Open an alert to see what raised its score, the payment behind it and the accounts involved. Then record a decision and the reason for it.

  • Scores rank from 0 to 1; they are never probabilities
  • Reviewing, Escalate or Close, with a note and a name
  • Escalating opens a case with the evidence attached

05 Account networks

Follow the money, hop by hop.

The account network shows who sent money to whom. Uploaded datasets add the laundering networks the model found, and you can trace a dated chain from one account to another.

  • Pass-through, fan-in, fan-out, round-trip and chain patterns
  • Flagged transfers in red, a traced trail in teal
  • Save the trail to a case as evidence

06 Cases and reporting

Evidence that holds up.

Alerts, notes and evidence come together in an encrypted case. The report leaves signed, with goAML XML, and the audit trail records every change.

  • Case workspace encrypted with AES-256-GCM behind a passphrase
  • Reports carry a SHA-256 fingerprint and an ECDSA P-256 signature
  • Anyone can verify a report file or the audit trail’s integrity
