How it works

From signal to investigation-ready evidence.

Four steps, one workspace. Machine learning and written rules surface the risk; an analyst reviews it, follows the money and decides what happens next.

  1. 01

    Detect

    Three sources raise alerts. Acurion K7 checks live payments, the core model scores uploaded bank files, and the rules engine checks the case workspace’s customers and payments.

    • Everything lands in one queue, highest risk first
    • K7 alerts pop up in the header pill as they arrive
    • Each alert names where it came from
  2. 02

    Review

    Open an alert to see its score, what raised it, the payment itself, both accounts and the customer behind them.

    • Reasons in plain language, strongest first
    • Scores rank from 0 to 1; they are never probabilities
    • Technical detail waits behind More info
  3. 03

    Investigate

    Follow the accounts linked to the alert, trace a dated chain from one account to another and save what you find to a case.

    • Laundering patterns from uploads are drawn on the map
    • Due diligence and screening sit in the same workspace
    • Evidence is attached to the case, not copied around
  4. 04

    Report

    Record the decision and the reason. An escalated alert becomes a case, and a case becomes a signed report with goAML XML, ready for a person to file.

    • Every change goes into a hash-chained audit trail
    • Anyone can verify the report’s signature later
    • Filing happens through the FIU’s own portal
