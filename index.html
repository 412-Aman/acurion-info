01 The challenge

Financial activity is connected. Investigations should be too.

Suspicious activity is hard to see when payments, customer context and account links sit in separate places. An analyst needs more than a flag: the reasons, the evidence and a way to record the decision.

Acurion puts the monitoring signals and the investigation tools in one workspace, so an analyst can move from an alert to a documented review without leaving it.

03 The workflow

04 Responsible use

Technology supports decisions. People remain accountable.

Acurion is an investigation-support tool. It ranks and explains; an analyst decides.

Human review

An analyst records the decision and the reason for every alert reviewed.

Evidence-led reporting

Reports are prepared for review. Filing happens separately, through the FIU’s own portal.

Acurion does not

  • Decide that laundering has happened
  • Move or stop real funds
  • Send reports to regulators on its own
